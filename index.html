How we work

A considered process, from first visit to lasting maintenance.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    Understand your property, lifestyle and requirements.

  2. 02

    Site Assessment

    Study space, sunlight, soil, drainage and existing landscape.

  3. 03

    Concept Design

    Develop the landscape concept and visual direction.

  4. 04

    Detailed Design

    Plants, materials, hardscape and outdoor elements.

  5. 05

    Execution

    Professional implementation and coordination on site.

  6. 06

    Maintenance

    Ongoing care to keep the landscape healthy and beautiful.
