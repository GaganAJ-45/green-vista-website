## Process

- Small line: How we work
- Heading: A considered process, from first visit to lasting maintenance.

1. **Consultation**: Understand your property, lifestyle and requirements.
2. **Site Assessment**: Study space, sunlight, soil, drainage and existing landscape.
3. **Concept Design**: Develop the landscape concept and visual direction.
4. **Detailed Design**: Plants, materials, hardscape and outdoor elements.
5. **Execution**: Professional implementation and coordination on site.
6. **Maintenance**: Ongoing care to keep the landscape healthy and beautiful.
